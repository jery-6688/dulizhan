About XINPUREAO

Where water purity meets engineering.

XINPUREAO is the filtration division of a leading water purification manufacturer, headquartered in China's renowned "Capital of Water Filters". Our integrated facility spans R&D, precision molding, and automated assembly — delivering shower filters, faucet purifiers, and replacement cartridges to 60+ countries.

Every filter is engineered with medical-grade materials, finished with proprietary multi-stage filtration technology, and certified to ISO 9001:2015. Built for global OEM/ODM partners who demand consistency.
