About XINPUREAO

A shower filter manufacturer dedicated to healthy shower water. 15 years, 60+ countries, and a 25,000 m² factory of in-house production.

Our Story

Engineering healthy shower water since 2010

XINPUREAO was founded by a team of water-treatment engineers with a simple belief: the quality of shower water matters as much as the quality of drinking water. Today we design, manufacture, and export to more than 500 brand and distributor partners in 60+ countries.

Our 25,000 m² production facility runs 18 injection machines, 3 assembly shifts, and an in-house R&D and QC lab — so we control quality, cost, and lead time under one roof.
